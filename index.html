Meet the founder

Rayan Khattar

Career Partner - Product Manager - Project Manager

I left Lebanon with one suitcase and a lot of uncertainty. No mapped-out plan, no five-year roadmap. Just an instinct that staying still was more dangerous than moving into the unknown. What followed was five years of building myself, piece by piece, across countries, setbacks, and a few moments I would not wish on anyone.

There were stretches of real depression. A pandemic that froze the world just as I was finding my footing. A natural disaster in the city I had fallen in love with. A war touching my homeland from thousands of miles away. A job market that kept saying no. An automated system that rejected my application before a human ever read it.

Between the setbacks, I was building obsessively. A master's degree in technology and engineering management, written in Spain, with a thesis on AI before it became everyone's talking point. Certifications stacked with intention. Months spent studying hundreds of job descriptions. Not to copy them, but to understand exactly what the market needed and how to speak that language authentically.

I became obsessed with growth. Self-coaching. Writing as both a creative and professional discipline. Productivity as a daily practice. New tools adopted until they became part of how I operate.

And through all of it, I kept showing up for the people around me. Helping friends rewrite their stories, land interviews, find direction. That pattern became impossible to ignore. It became a calling.

"I have lived the rejections and the breakthroughs, from both sides. What I offer is not a template. It is the clarity and honesty I wish someone had given me."

That is what Lodestar is. Not a service. A commitment. Your story, told properly, from the first draft to the day you accept the offer.
